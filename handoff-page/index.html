What still fails
  • A right answer that garbles or narrows its sentence.
  • A wrong option as close to the text as the right one, or a paraphrase of a nearby sentence that entailment misses.
  • Two wrong options making one claim; a wrong option that is a right one plus "is optional".
  • Trivial key sentences ("humans build software"), and stand-ins such as "a homeless person" for a named character.
The pipeline
  1. Passage. Contents pages, licences, quotation lists and references are refused.
  2. Key sentences. Code picks what is tested: one whole sentence ranked against the section summary, or 2 to 3 items of one list.
  3. Question. The writer phrases it. Refused if the answer misses the key's words, it names someone the passage doesn't, points at its source, says "someone", or the stem states the answer. One retry with the reason.
  4. Wrong options. Dropped if copied, absolute when the right one isn't, the wrong length, first person, an echo of an untested list item, or entailed by the passage or the five nearest chunks.
  5. Judge answers with the passage and must pick exactly the right answers.
  6. Guesser answers without it in three orders; right every time refuses the question at medium and hard.
Moving it
  1. git fetch && git worktree add ../lm-quiz-engine feature/quiz-engine in the Luminary checkout.
  2. Copy lm-quiz-verify-scripts. With downloads allowed, fetch facebook/bart-large-mnli and the chosen NLI models into <library>/models/.
  3. Copy lm-quiz-verify-data/luminary.db and vectors/, or ingest the three books and update the document ids in run_build.py.
  4. Check every document's vector count equals its chunk count.
